Dawnhold

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Dawnhold is made by Walde-Purdik. This page says what the app does with your information.

In short

Dawnhold has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code. We do not collect anything about you, and we have no server that your game is sent to.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Your game

Your tasks, habits, buildings, quests and record are saved on your phone. If you use iCloud, a copy is kept in your own iCloud account so that a new phone or a reinstall can pick the game up again. We cannot see that copy.

Location

The app uses your location to show your base on the map, to find quests near you and to notice when you reach a quest or a place one of your tasks leads to. If you allow location "Always", it can notice that while the app is closed. Your location is used on your phone. The places you choose (your base, your outposts, the places tasks lead to) are part of your saved game.

Apple Maps

Finding places and walking routes is done by Apple Maps, so those requests, which include a location, go to Apple. Apple's privacy policy covers them.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, the app reads the day's steps, active calories and stand hours to finish step and calorie quests and tasks you have given a goal. It never writes to Health. The counts stay on your phone: they are left out of the copy of your game in iCloud. Health information is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone.

Notifications

Reminders and reports are planned on your phone. Nothing is sent to us to make them work.

Your choices

You can turn off location, Apple Health or notifications for Dawnhold in the Settings app at any time. The parts of the game that need them stop working and the rest carries on.

Deleting the app deletes the game on your phone. The copy in iCloud is in your own account, where only you can reach it. You can stop Dawnhold from using iCloud in Settings, under your name and iCloud.

Children

Dawnhold does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
